:tada: Congrats to my former RA **Baixin Li** on his first diffusion language model (DLM) watermarking paper accepted to **EMNLP 2026 Findings**, titled with _"SAC-Copula: Quality-Preserving Watermarking for Diffusion Language Models via Smooth Correlated Gumbel Fields"_. We study the problem of watermarking DLMs, which requires mechanisms compatible with iterative parallel unmasking rather than autoregressive decoding. We propose SAC-Copula, a quality-preserving watermarking method for DLMs based on smooth, locally correlated Gumbel perturbation fields constructed via a Gaussian copula.
